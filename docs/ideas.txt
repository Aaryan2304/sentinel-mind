### 🌊 1. **"Dark Vessel" Narrative Forensics for IUU Fishing Enforcement**
**Problem**: Illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing vessels often disable AIS transponders ("go dark"). While optical detection exists , analysts still manually review thousands of detections to distinguish fishing activity from legitimate transit.

**Unique VLM Application**: Fine-tune a compact VLM (e.g., Qwen2-VL-2B) to generate *investigative narratives* from Sentinel-2 detections:
- **Input**: Bi-temporal Sentinel-2 patches + AIS metadata (or lack thereof)
- **Prompt**: *"This vessel was detected at [coords] on [date] without AIS. Analyze wake patterns, proximity to protected zones, and vessel type. Is this likely IUU fishing? Explain step-by-step."*
- **Output**: Structured reasoning + confidence score + recommended action

**Why it's unique**:
- Moves beyond binary detection to *investigative reasoning*—aligning with your CoT/world model interests 
- Leverages Sentinel-2's 10m resolution to detect subtle cues (net deployment patterns, transshipment behavior) 
- Deployable via Skylight's API to NGOs/governments already using Sentinel-2 for maritime surveillance 

**Efficiency angle**: Use 4-bit quantization + LoRA on vision encoder only (spectral bands are domain-shifted from ImageNet) to fit on edge devices for coastal patrol vessels.

**Why it's unique**:

**Why it's unique**:

**Unique VLM Application**: A VLM that *converses with farmers* to resolve satellite-ground discrepancies:
- **Input**: Sentinel-2 time series + farmer-submitted photo + claim text ("My maize failed due to drought")
- **Prompt**: *"The satellite shows healthy vegetation in this field during the claimed drought period. The farmer's photo shows wilting. Reconcile these observations. What is the most likely cause?"*
- **Output**: Discrepancy analysis + recommended action (approve/reject/request field visit)

**Why it's unique**:
- Turns VLMs into *mediators* between satellite data and human context—underexplored in remote sensing VLM literature
- Uses Sentinel-2's SWIR bands to detect water stress invisible in RGB 
- Directly addresses a $3B+ market (agricultural insurance in emerging economies)

**Efficiency angle**: Fine-tune only the LLM head with LoRA; use cached spectral embeddings for common crop types to minimize VRAM.
